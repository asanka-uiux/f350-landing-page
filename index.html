The build story

Building a work truck
the right way.

Custom Offsets Build Team . 5 Min Read

Most build videos start with a shiny truck that already looks pretty nice. This one starts with a 2019 F-350 XLT 6.7L that was bought to work, which means it came with steel wheels, a plastic bumper, carpet nobody protected and a bed that had been open to the weather its whole life. That is what most F-350s in this country actually look like.

So the question was not how to make it the coolest truck at a show. It was simpler and more useful: if you own a truck that works for a living, what should you buy first, what can wait, and what should you skip entirely?

Why categories

We ordered everything at once and let it stack up at HQ, and by the time it was all in there were parts stacked across the shop floor. Grouping them into eight categories is how we made sense of it, and it is how the page is built: suspension, wheels and tires, bumpers, lighting, bed cover, towing and steps, interior, performance.

Open any one of them and you get the same three things. What is in it. Why you would want it. And who should skip it, because not every category on this list belongs on every truck, and a page that pretends otherwise is just a catalog.

What we would buy first

If somebody handed us this truck and we could only do one category, we would take the interior. Liners and seat covers are the difference between a cab that cleans up in five years and one that never does. Nobody makes a video about floor liners. Everybody wishes they had bought them.

If we could do two, the second would be performance. The moment you put a taller, heavier tire on a truck that already weighs what an F-350 weighs, stopping gets harder before anything else gets better. The PowerStop kit is the one that matters most in that category, and it goes on before anything that adds power.

Why now

Nobody is suggesting you do all eight at once. That is the point of breaking it into categories: each one stands on its own, and you can work through them in whatever order your truck argues for. It is also worth saying out loud that this lands right before the end of the year, so if you have things to put on the books before Uncle Sam takes his cut, this is a better use than most. The financing block above is there if you would rather spread it out.

Fitment, before you order anything

Every part on this page is specified for a 2017 to 2022 Super Duty unless the card says otherwise, and most of these parts are year and cab specific. Run your own truck through the fitment gallery before you buy, because an F-250 and an F-350 do not always take the same part, and a 2019 does not always take what a 2023 does.
