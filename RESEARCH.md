Two things to watch.

First, the build sheet says $3,153.00 for the package and our site says $3,745.80.
The page uses our site, same as the other three conflicts above.

Second, the wheel price is a Black Friday sale price. The site is showing 20 percent
off Method right now, so $1,692.80 will go back to $2,116.00 when the sale ends and
the package will go back toward $4,189.00. The two bumpers are in the same position.
Worth a sweep of every struck-through price on this page before launch.

The tire also appears on the tire store listing at $259.13 each, which does not match
the $518.25 on its own product page. I used the product page.
