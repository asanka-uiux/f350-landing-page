HOW TO REVIEW
1. Unzip this folder somewhere, keeping index.html, styles.css, script.js and the
   images folder together.
2. Double click index.html. It opens in any browser. No server, no install.
3. The category section in the middle is interactive. The big tab is the broadcast
   banner itself: brands in the red bar, the name of the category under it. Click it
   and the panel below opens: the four checked specs in a row, then the photo with
   why you want it and who should skip it beside it. The square tabs either side
   switch categories, arrow keys work too.
